# Fact-check, 29 October 2026

A second, web-assisted pass over all 40 styles and 120 works (1,291 claims). Each claim was checked against live sources in the usual order: the holding museum's record first, then museum, academic and official publications, then major encyclopedias. Wikipedia was used only to find better sources. Claims that were not settled were checked again by a stronger reviewer with a wider search, and then a third time by an independent reviewer.

## Checked and kept

Four lineage edits the first pass proposed were checked again and kept as they are. The atlas's "Grew out of" links can only point at styles in the atlas, and the sources support the existing links:

- Shan shui → Ukiyo-e (both directions): Hokusai and Hiroshige drew on Chinese landscape models (the *Mustard Seed Garden Manual*, the Eight Views theme). Sources: NGV; British Museum; MFA Boston.
- Pop Art from Dada: Duchamp's readymades are a documented source for Pop. Neo-Dada is the bridge, but it has no entry here. Source: Britannica.
- Pop Art from Abstract Expressionism: Pop defined itself against it, and that still counts as growing out of it. Source: Tate.
